## Findings that shape the design
- **Launches:** 49.0% of returning clients' test positives are on products first reviewed inside C. Collaborative filtering cannot score them, so evaluation reports a catalogue slice and a launch slice.
- **Sampled reviews:** 13.7% of reviews mention a free or gifted product (a lower bound from a text rule). They are excluded from positives.
- **Snapshot leakage:** product_info `rating`, `reviews` and `loves_count` are March 2023 values. They are never used as features.
- **Survivorship:** products delisted before March 2023 are absent.
